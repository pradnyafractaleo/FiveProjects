Nugget Feed is a personal, non-commercial page that points to interesting things on the web. Every card links back to where it came from. We don't host or claim ownership of anyone else's content.

Sources

  • Wikipedia: short intro excerpts, used under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each card links to the original article and its contributors.
  • Hacker News: story titles and links, via the HN Search API.
  • DEV Community: article titles, short descriptions and author names, via the Forem API. The articles belong to their authors.

Images

Card images are AI-generated by Pollinations.ai based on each title. They are decorative illustrations, not photos of the real subject.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, cookies, ads or analytics. Your browser loads content directly from the sources above, from Pollinations (card titles are sent to it as image prompts) and from Google Fonts. Each of these sees your IP address. Admin settings are kept only in your own browser.

Removal requests

If you'd like something taken down, please open an issue on GitHub.
